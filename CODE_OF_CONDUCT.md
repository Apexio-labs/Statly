# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, insults and
publishing someone's private details are not accepted here. That last point matters on a project
whose contributors may prefer to stay pseudonymous.

To report a problem, use the private reporting form under the repository's **Security** tab or write
to statly.anki@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments and
code, and ban people who break these rules.
