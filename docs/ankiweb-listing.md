**Title:** Statly: your own statistics dashboard

---

<b>A statistics dashboard for Anki that you arrange yourself.</b>

Drag and resize widgets, filter by time range and deck, and choose a theme and a background. Anki's own statistics are all there as widgets, with Anki's numbers and FSRS values, next to widgets of your own that you build without code.

<b>On your phone too.</b> Statly can put your dashboard on a card that travels with the normal Anki sync, arranged for phones and tablets. No server is involved.

<b>An assistant, if you want one.</b> Ask about your learning with your own API key or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. It can never change your cards, needs your permission to read them, and every suggestion waits for your click. Connections to cloud services are still marked as beta.

<b>Your data stays yours.</b> Statly reads your collection and changes nothing in it, apart from its own phone card if you switch that on. There is no telemetry, no account and no server.

<b>Requirements:</b> Anki 25.09 or newer.

<b>Open source.</b> Statly is released under the MIT License. The code is on <a href="https://github.com/apexio-labs/statly">GitHub</a>.

<b>Found a bug?</b> Please report it on <a href="https://github.com/apexio-labs/statly/issues">GitHub</a>, not in the reviews, so it can be answered.
